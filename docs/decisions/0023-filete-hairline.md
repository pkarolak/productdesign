# 0023: The filete flourish becomes a hairline

- **Status:** Accepted. Replaces the flourish of [ADR 0019](0019-filete-lettering.md).
- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Todo:** owner feedback on dividers

## Context

The owner did not like the scrolled filete flourish under headings and asked for something more subtle, or nothing.

## Decision

`Filete` is now a short accent hairline, 24px wide and 1px tall, under the hero headline, section headings and the About teaser headline. The painted shade on one hero phrase and on headings remains the fileteado nod.

## Alternatives considered

- Removing it: the headings would lose a quiet anchor between title and body.
- A small diamond between hairlines: still ornamental.
