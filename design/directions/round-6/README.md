# Round 6: story first, work first

Round 5 was rejected for putting the hobbies ahead of the work. The new brief: the work leads, and the four passions (climbing, tango, running, tango DJ) become a story about how they make the designs better. The look must be work-first, not hobby-styled, and not borrowed from benshih.design.

**Story shape:** the hero states the premise ("Hi! I'm Patryk. I design products in the moments free from climbing and tango."). Then four scroll-driven chapters, each an analogy pinned on the left with its case evidence arriving on the right.

**Status:** proposed, awaiting a pick.

## Notes for the build

- The comps invent company and product names; the build uses the existing fictional content.
- A shows all four cases in the hero; B shows one case in a tabbed window. Either pattern can move to the other direction.
- Whichever direction wins, it also needs its opposite mode (light for B, dark for A).
