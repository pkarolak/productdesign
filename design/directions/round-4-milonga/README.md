# Round 4: Milonga (personal direction)

A second design language, built alongside Blueprint as its own theme (`themes/milonga/`) so either can be switched on. Brief: the warmth and personality of [benshih.design](https://www.benshih.design/), plus the energy of a runner, climber, tango dancer and tango DJ. Personality wraps the work (hero, interludes, approach, off the clock, contact); case pages stay crisp.

**Status:** rejected. Too close to benshih.design in palette, type, handwriting and the fanned cards. Replaced by [round 5](../round-5/README.md).

## Concept

The page is a night at a milonga. Four cases are "a tanda"; short full-bleed interludes between sections are "cortinas"; the contact block is a "cabeceo", the nod that invites a dance.

- **Palette:** cream paper `#F7F1E8`, ink `#1F1A17`, wine `#8E2433` for display type, and one color per passion on cards only: apricot `#FFC9A3` (running), sage `#CFE0C3` (climbing), rose `#F4C6CF` (tango), lilac `#D9D0F5` (DJ).
- **Type:** a chunky expressive grotesk for display (Bricolage Grotesque direction), a humanist sans for body (Figtree direction), and a handwritten accent (Caveat direction) for small notes only.
- **Shape and motion:** big rounded containers, tilted cards like a fanned hand, snappier motion with a little overshoot.
